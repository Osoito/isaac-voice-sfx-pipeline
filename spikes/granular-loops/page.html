Isaac voice SFX mod · design spike

Can a human voice hold a loop?

Six candidates for the two hardest targets in the mod, built by granular synthesis from single sustained vowels. Every clip below is 2–3 repetitions baked into one file, so the join is audible without relying on gapless playback. Compare each against the vanilla reference row.

Result

The synthesised loops join more cleanly than the game’s own. Grains are written with a modulo-N index, so a grain crossing the end wraps to the start and the buffer is seamless by construction. Vanilla’s two files were cut by hand and are not.

What the seam number means

The jump between the last and first sample, divided by the waveform’s own 99.9th-percentile step. At or below 1.00 the join is indistinguishable from ordinary motion inside the wave.

Why the wrap panel is drawn

12 ms of tail followed by 12 ms of head, in playback order. A seamless loop reads as one continuous line across the marked centre; a broken one shows a step exactly there.

Level, and what it cost

Each candidate is driven to the vanilla file’s RMS under a 6 dB limiting budget. portal_loop is a hot file at 0.426 RMS; the budget runs out about 3 dB short, and the shortfall is reported rather than hidden.

What to listen for

Whether it reads as a texture or as a person. Grain size sets that: short grains shred the voice into noise, long ones let syllable identity leak back through.
